About SmartEval

A web-based platform built to structure and streamline the online testing lifecycle.

SmartEval is a web-based examination and evaluation platform designed to simplify the process of conducting and managing online examinations.

The system provides structured workflows for both instructors and learners, ensuring that tests are easily scheduled, attempted digitally, and securely recorded without complex manual overhead.

Basic Examination Workflow

1

Create & Manage

Teacher or Admin defines examination details and questions.

2

Schedule

Test date, time, and timing constraints are finalized.

3

Participate

Students access and attempt the scheduled test digitally.

4

Submit

Responses are recorded directly in the platform.

5

Evaluate

Evaluation and results are handled through the system.

About The Project

Academic context and background of SmartEval.

Academic Project Objective

SmartEval is developed as an academic and project-based online examination and evaluation system with the objective of understanding and implementing the complete digital examination workflow.

The project focuses on creating an intuitive, role-segregated environment where digital assessments can be structured, attempted, and managed efficiently using standard web technologies.
